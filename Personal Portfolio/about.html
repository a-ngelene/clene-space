About Me!

Hi, I'm Carla! Welcome to clene space. I've been drawing basically my whole life. I used to just sketch dresses back in the day because I was so convinced I was going to be a fashion designer.

I got into portraits around 2018, and I still take commissions from friends and family when my schedule allows it. Graphite is my go-to, but I also dabble in digital art, acrylics, and oil pastels.

Art has always been my safe space and my main outlet. But TBH, ever since college started, I completely lost the time for it. That's actually why I built this site, I needed a passion project to force me out of my art slump and give my works a proper home.

When I'm not doing art things, I'm surviving as a 3rd-year CS student. And I also do some student leadership stuff on the side. But outside of acads, you'll probably just find me hanging out with my cats, crocheting, or hyperfixating on fantasy and historical dramas nowadays.
